# Highlight & Ask

In long research reports or detailed code explanations, asking a follow-up question often requires quoting a specific paragraph. Klecx streamlines this with **Highlight & Ask**.

## How It Works

1. **Select Any Text**: In any rendered response card, use your mouse or trackpad to highlight a phrase, sentence, or table row.
2. **Context Tooltip**: A floating action menu appears next to your selection with the option **"Ask about this"**.
3. **Automatic Context Quoting**: Clicking the button automatically pins the highlighted excerpt into your composer as a blockquote, allowing you to ask questions like:
   - *"Can you explain this part in simpler terms?"*
   - *"Provide a TypeScript implementation for this step."*
   - *"Are there any edge cases with this recommendation?"*

## Robust Text Anchoring

Klecx uses the W3C **TextQuoteSelector** standard behind the scenes. Even if responses are re-rendered or formatted, your highlighted anchor points remain accurately referenced in the conversation log.
