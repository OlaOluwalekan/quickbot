# Getting Started with Klecx

Klecx is a workspace for working with AI on research, planning, writing and everyday projects. Each topic gets its own workspace, and every answer stays where you can read it, edit it, question it and share it.

## 1. Your First Workspace

After you sign in, you land on the home page. Type what you want to work on in the composer and press `Enter` (`Shift` + `Enter` adds a new line). Klecx creates a workspace for it and opens it.

In the composer you can:

- **Choose a model**: **Fast & Efficient** (≈ {{credits.fast}} credits for a typical message) for everyday questions, or **Standard Reasoning** (≈ {{credits.standard}} credits) for complex research and multi-step work. Each option shows its typical cost before you send. Standard Reasoning is available from the Lite plan.
- **Create images**: switch the composer to **Create Image** (≈ {{credits.image}} credits per image, from the Lite plan).
- **Attach files**: add images, PDFs and documents to a message. You can also add a document to the workspace itself (see [Workspace documents](/help/documents)).
- **Use your voice**: record a message instead of typing it (see [Voice input and web search](/help/voice-and-web-search)).

Klecx searches the web on its own when a question needs current information. You can turn this off in **Settings → AI preferences**.

## 2. Working with Answers

Each answer appears as a card under your prompt:

- **Thought process**: when the model reasons before answering, you can open a short summary of its thinking.
- **Copy, Edit, Regenerate**: copy the text, correct the answer yourself, or ask for a new version. Earlier versions are kept (see [Regenerate vs Edit](/help/regenerate-vs-edit)).
- **Comment and Ask**: select any passage to leave a comment or ask a side question about it (see [Highlight & Ask](/help/highlight-and-ask)).
- **Export and Share**: download an answer, or create a public link to it.

## 3. Organizing Your Work

The sidebar lists your projects and workspaces. Group related workspaces into a **project**: each project has its own page with all its workspaces, and the people you invite to a project can work in every workspace inside it (see [Workspaces vs Projects](/help/workspaces-vs-projects)). Use **Search** (`Ctrl` + `K`, or `⌘` + `K` on Mac) to jump to any workspace.
