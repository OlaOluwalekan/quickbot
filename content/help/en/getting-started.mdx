# Getting Started with Klecx

Welcome to **Klecx**, a fast, private, and flexible workspace for collaborating with modern AI models. Whether you are drafting documents, analyzing data, researching complex topics, or exploring creative ideas, Klecx provides a clean and distraction-free environment.

## 1. Creating Your First Workspace

When you log into Klecx, you land on the **Workspace Home** (`/workspaces`). Here you can immediately begin typing in the composer at the bottom of the screen.

- **Prompt Input**: Type any question, code request, or writing task into the composer.
- **Model Selector**: Switch between Economy (`Gemini Flash`) for quick iterations or Standard (`Gemini Pro`) for deep analysis and nuanced tasks.
- **Submitting**: Press `Enter` (or `Cmd` + `Enter` / `Ctrl` + `Enter`) to start your conversation.

Your prompt is smoothly preserved as the application transitions into your dedicated workspace URL (`/workspaces/:id`).

## 2. Navigating the Conversation

Each AI response in Klecx is presented as an interactive response card:

- **Thinking Panel**: If the model reasons through a complex prompt, you can see its live thinking summary collapsible accordion.
- **Copy & Actions**: Copy markdown or rendered output with one click using the clipboard button.
- **Regenerate & Versioning**: Request alternate takes or model perspectives without losing previous responses.
- **In-place Edits**: Edit your prompts or responses directly to explore branches and refine outcomes.

## 3. Organizing with Projects

As your workspaces grow, group them into **Projects** to keep your client work, coding experiments, and personal research organized. Use the sidebar to switch workspaces, pin favorites, or search across conversations.
