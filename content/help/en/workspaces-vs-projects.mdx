# Workspaces vs Projects

Klecx has two levels of organization: **workspaces** hold the work, and **projects** group related workspaces.

## What is a Workspace?

A workspace is one conversation about one topic. It holds:

- Your prompts and Klecx's answers, with every version of each answer.
- Comments and side questions on passages of those answers.
- Up to {{documents.max}} workspace documents, which Klecx reads with every prompt.
- Klecx notes: corrections you asked Klecx to remember in this workspace.
- Its own members and public links.

Workspaces don't have to belong to a project. Start one for a quick question and move it into a project later.

## What is a Project?

A project is a folder for related workspaces, for example:

- **Café Launch**: *Menu pricing*, *Opening week plan*, *Supplier emails*.
- **Biology Exam Prep**: *Cell biology*, *Genetics*, *Revision schedule*.

Click a project in the sidebar to open its page. It lists all of its workspaces, and you can create a new workspace in the project from there.

## People and Permissions

You can invite people to a single workspace or to a whole project. Someone invited to a project gets the same role in every workspace of that project. A workspace can give a person more rights than the project does, never fewer. See [Collaboration & Roles](/help/collaboration-roles).

## When to Create a Project

Create a project when:

1. A goal needs several separate conversations.
2. You want to give the same people access to a group of workspaces at once.
3. You want to keep your sidebar tidy.
