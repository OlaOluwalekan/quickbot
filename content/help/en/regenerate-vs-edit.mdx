# Regenerate vs Edit

When an AI response isn't quite right or when you want to explore an alternative phrasing, Klecx offers two distinct workflows: **Regeneration** and **Inline Editing**.

## 1. Regenerating a Response

Clicking the **Regenerate** button at the footer of any response card requests a completely new generation from the AI model while retaining the original prompt and context.

- **Non-destructive Versioning**: Klecx never discards your previous outputs. When you regenerate, a version indicator appears (e.g., `2 / 2`).
- **Version Navigation**: Use the <kbd>Prev</kbd> and <kbd>Next</kbd> buttons (or keyboard arrow keys <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd>) to switch between versions seamlessly.
- **Model Switching**: You can choose a different model (such as switching from Economy to Standard) when regenerating to compare responses side-by-side.

## 2. Editing an Existing Response

Sometimes the AI gets 95% of an answer right, but needs a slight correction or customized code snippet. Rather than spending credits on a full regeneration, click **Edit Response**.

- **Inline Markdown Editor**: Modify the text directly with real-time preview.
- **Audit History**: Edited responses show an "Edited" badge so team members know adjustments were made.
- **View Original**: You can toggle between your custom edits and the AI's original raw text at any time.

## 3. Editing Previous Prompts

If you want to change what you asked earlier in the thread, you can edit your original prompt. Klecx branches the conversation from that point, allowing you to explore alternate conversation paths without losing the previous branch.
