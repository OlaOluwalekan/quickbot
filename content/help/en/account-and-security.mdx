# Account & Security

Protecting your conversations and account access is foundational to Klecx.

## Password & Reset Protocol

- **Strong Password Requirement**: Klecx requires passwords to be at least 10 characters long, containing a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
- **Session Revocation on Reset**: Whenever you successfully reset your password via email recovery, **all other active sessions across all devices are immediately revoked** to protect you against unauthorized access.

## Active Sessions

In **Settings → Sessions**, you can review every browser and device currently connected to your account:
- Device type, operating system, and browser name.
- Approximate IP address and geographic location.
- Last active timestamp.
- **Log out other sessions**: Terminate all remote sessions with one click while staying logged in on your current device.

## Data Retention & Deletion

- **Soft-Deleted Conversations**: When you delete a workspace, it is placed in your trash bin for 30 days, after which all database records and attached assets are permanently erased.
- **Complete Account Deletion**: You can request full account deletion from **Settings → Profile**. All personal information, attachments in Cloudinary, credit records, and workspaces will be purged in compliance with GDPR and CCPA standards.
