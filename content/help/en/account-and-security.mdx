# Account & Security

## Signing In and Passwords

- You can sign in with an email address and password, or with one of the other accounts offered on the sign-in page.
- Passwords must be at least 10 characters long. A longer passphrase is the safest choice.
- To change a forgotten password, choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the link in the email. Resetting your password signs you out on every other device.
- Password reset emails are limited to 3 requests per hour.

Your email address can't be changed from Settings. Your username can be chosen once and then stays the same.

## Sessions & Devices

**Settings → Sessions & Devices** lists every device signed in to your account, with:

- The browser and operating system.
- A partly hidden IP address and the country, when known.
- When it was last active.

You can sign out a single device, or all other devices at once. Signing out all other devices asks for your password if you haven't signed in recently.

## Sign-in Alerts

When your account is used from a new browser, operating system or country, Klecx sends you a notification and an email. If it wasn't you, sign out that device in **Sessions & Devices** and reset your password. You can change which alerts you receive in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Deleting Your Data

- **Workspaces**: archive a workspace to hide it, or delete it. Deleting asks you to confirm and can't be undone.
- **Your account**: account deletion isn't available in Settings yet. To delete your account or to get a copy of your data, write to %%PRIVACY_EMAIL%% from the email address of your account.
