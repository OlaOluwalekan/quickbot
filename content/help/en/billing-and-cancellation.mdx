# Billing & Cancellation

Klecx partners with **Creem** to provide secure, PCI-compliant payment processing, recurring billing, and invoice generation.

## Managing Your Subscription

Navigate to **Settings → Billing** to:
- View your current plan (Free, Pro, or Max) and next renewal date.
- Review past invoices and download PDF receipts.
- Update your credit card or billing details.
- Upgrade or downgrade plan tiers with prorated credit calculation.

## How Cancellation Works

You can cancel your subscription at any time with one click directly inside the app:
1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Confirm your cancellation.

**What happens after cancellation?**
- You retain full access to your paid plan benefits and remaining monthly credits until the end of your current billing cycle.
- At the end of the period, your account smoothly converts to the Free tier.
- Your workspaces, notes, and custom versions remain completely intact and accessible.

## Refund Policy

If you were billed in error or experienced technical outages preventing the use of your credits, please contact our support team at `support@mail.klecx.vyraxity.com` within 14 days of the charge. We review refund requests individually with fairness and prompt resolution.
