# Sharing

A public link lets anyone read a workspace or a single answer without an account. To work together on a workspace instead, invite people (see [Collaboration & Roles](/help/collaboration-roles)).

## Creating a Public Link

1. Open the workspace and click **Share** in the top bar, or **Share** on an answer.
2. Open the **Public Link** tab and choose:
   - **Share scope**: **Entire workspace** or **This response only**.
   - **Version to display**: **Edited if available** or **Original AI output**.
   - **Expiration**: none, 7 days or 30 days.
   - **Show my name on the public page**, if you want visitors to see who shared it.
3. Click **Create public link**. The link is copied to your clipboard. It is shown only once, so keep it somewhere safe.

Creating public links needs the Admin role or above.

## What Visitors See

- The prompts and answers you chose, as they are **now**: later edits and new answers in a shared workspace appear on the public page too.
- **Never included**: comments, side questions, Klecx notes, members and edit history. For documents, only the text is shown; the original file can't be downloaded from a public link.
- Public pages are not indexed by search engines, but anyone who has the link can open it. Don't share content you need to keep private.

## Revoking a Link

Open **Share → Public Link** and click **Revoke** next to the link. It stops working immediately. Revoked and expired links show a "not found" page, the same as a link that never existed.
