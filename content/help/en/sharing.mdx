# Sharing Workspaces

Klecx allows you to share valuable AI research, code explanations, and documentation with teammates, clients, or the public via secure read-only links.

## Creating a Share Link

1. Open the workspace you want to share.
2. Click the **Share** button located in the top navigation bar.
3. Choose your sharing permissions:
   - **Public Read-Only Link**: Anyone with the link can view the conversation.
   - **Snapshot vs Live**: Choose whether visitors see the conversation as it exists today (snapshot) or see future responses in real time.
4. Click **Create Link** and copy the generated URL (`/s/:token`).

## Important Security Information

- **URL Access**: Public share links are accessible to anyone who possesses the exact link URL. Avoid pasting confidential credentials or proprietary secrets into conversations before sharing.
- **Search Engine Indexing**: All public share routes (`/s/*`) explicitly transmit `noindex, nofollow` robot headers to prevent search engines from indexing your shared conversations.

## Revoking a Share Link

You maintain total control over your shared content. At any time:
1. Re-open the **Share** dialog.
2. Click **Revoke Link**.
3. The URL immediately stops serving content and displays an expiration notice to subsequent visitors.
