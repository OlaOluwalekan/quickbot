# Language Settings

Klecx has two separate language settings: the language of the interface, and the language Klecx answers in.

## Interface Language

Klecx is available in English, Spanish and French. Change the language with the language menu in the top bar, or in **Settings** under **Display Language**. Your choice is saved to your account and used on every device you sign in on. Notification emails use it too.

## Answer Language

In **Settings → AI preferences**, **Language Klecx answers in** decides the language of answers:

- **Auto (match my message)** (default): Klecx answers in the language you write in.
- **A fixed language**: Klecx always answers in that language, whatever language you write in.

This setting applies to your own prompts and side questions. Each person in a shared workspace uses their own setting.

## Help Articles

Help articles are shown in your interface language. An article that isn't translated yet is shown in English.
