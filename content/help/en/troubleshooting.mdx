# Troubleshooting & FAQs

If you run into an unexpected issue while using Klecx, this guide covers the most frequent questions and diagnostic steps.

## Generation Halts or "Thinking" Appears Stuck

If a generation shows a pulsing indicator but doesn't stream tokens after 15 seconds:
1. **Network Disconnection**: Check your internet connection. Server-sent events (SSE) require an uninterrupted streaming connection.
2. **Click Retry**: If a generation fails or drops, an inline **Retry** banner will appear. Clicking **Retry** generates a fresh idempotency key so you are never charged double credits.
3. **Model Outage**: Check our public status dashboard at [/status](/status) to see if Google Gemini or downstream infrastructure is experiencing elevated latency.

## "Insufficient Credits" Banner

If you receive a 402 Insufficient Credits notice:
- Your workspace prompt requires more credits than your current balance holds.
- Switch to an Economy model (`Gemini Flash`) to reduce credit requirements, or upgrade your plan in [/pricing](/pricing).

## Reset Password Link Not Received

If you requested a password recovery email but have not received it:
1. Check your spam and promotions folder.
2. Ensure you entered the exact email address used during account creation.
3. Note that password reset requests are rate-limited to 3 attempts per hour to protect accounts against automated abuse.

## Contacting Support

If your problem persists, submit a ticket via [/support](/support) or email our engineering team directly at `support@mail.klecx.vyraxity.com`. We respond within ≤ 3 business days.
