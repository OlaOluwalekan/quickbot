# Troubleshooting

## An Answer Fails or Stops

If an answer stops or shows an error:

1. **Check your connection.** Answers are written live; a dropped connection interrupts them.
2. **Click Try again.** If the answer failed before any text was written, nothing was charged; if it stopped part-way, only the written text was charged.
3. **Check the [status page](/status)** if errors keep happening.

## "Out of Credits" or the Daily Limit

- **Out of credits**: switch to **Fast & Efficient** to use fewer credits per message.
- **Daily limit reached**: the daily cap resets at midnight UTC. See [Credits & Plans](/help/credits-and-plans).
- **In someone else's workspace**: the owner's credits pay for answers. The message tells you whether the owner is out of credits or you have reached the monthly limit the owner set for you; ask the owner.

## "Web Search Limit Reached for Today"

Klecx answered without searching because your daily number of searches in this owner's workspaces is used up. Answers still work; searching is available again the next day. See [Voice input and web search](/help/voice-and-web-search).

## Voice Input Doesn't Work

- Allow the microphone for this site in your browser's settings, then reload the page.
- If the microphone button is greyed out, your browser can't record audio here; try an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- Recordings stop after 2 minutes, and each plan has a daily number of minutes.

## Password Reset Email Not Received

1. Check your spam and promotions folders.
2. Make sure you entered the email address of your account.
3. Reset emails are limited to 3 requests per hour; wait before trying again.

## Contacting Support

If the problem continues, use the [support form](/support) (you can attach screenshots) or write to %%SUPPORT_EMAIL%%. We respond within 3 business days.
