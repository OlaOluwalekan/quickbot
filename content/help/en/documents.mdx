# Workspace Documents

A workspace document is a file you work on together with Klecx: Klecx reads it with every prompt, you can comment on it and ask about it, ask Klecx to review it, correct it, and download the corrected version.

## Adding a Document

Drop a file on the composer, or attach it, and choose **Add to workspace as a document**. Choose **Attach to message** instead if you only want Klecx to read it for one message.

- **File types**: Markdown, plain text, Word (`.docx`) and CSV. A PDF can be attached to a message but can't be added as a document, because only its text can be read and it can't be downloaded again as a corrected PDF.
- **Up to {{documents.max}} documents per workspace.** Each one is sent with every prompt, so the limit keeps answers fast and their cost predictable.
- Klecx keeps the text of the document for reading, highlighting and editing, and keeps the original file for download.

## Asking Klecx to Review

Choose **Ask Klecx to review** on the document. Pick a focus or describe what to look for, and choose who sees the comments. Klecx reads the saved text and leaves comments on exact passages, shown with a wavy underline. The popover shows the most the review can cost before you start, and the workspace owner's credits are used once for the whole review.

## Accepting Fixes

Many review comments come with a suggested change:

1. Hover an underlined passage to see what is wrong and the change (old → new).
2. Choose **Accept** or **Dismiss** for that change, or **Accept all fixes** on the document to take every open fix at once.
3. Accepting changes your unsaved draft. Choose **Save** to keep the changes; until then you can still discard them.

If the passage changed in the meantime, the fix can no longer be applied and Klecx says so. While a document has unsaved changes you can't add comments or questions to it; save first.

## Editing and Downloading

Choose **Edit** to change the text yourself, then **Save**. **Download** offers:

- **Original file**: the file exactly as uploaded.
- **Edited version**: the saved text as Markdown, PDF or Word, depending on the workspace owner's plan (see [Exporting](/help/exporting)).

## Generated Documents

When you ask Klecx to write a document (a plan, a letter, a report), it opens in a panel next to the conversation. You can edit it, comment on it and ask about it, and download it like any other document.
