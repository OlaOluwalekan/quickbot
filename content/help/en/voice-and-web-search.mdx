# Voice Input and Web Search

## Voice Input

Click the microphone in the composer, speak, and click it again to stop. Klecx writes what you said into the composer so you can check it and edit it before sending.

- **Length**: up to 2 minutes per recording.
- **Daily limit**: a number of minutes per day, depending on your plan: {{plan.FREE.voiceMinutesPerDay}} on Free, {{plan.LITE.voiceMinutesPerDay}} on Lite, {{plan.STANDARD.voiceMinutesPerDay}} on Standard and {{plan.PRO.voiceMinutesPerDay}} on Pro.
- **Cost**: transcription uses a few credits from **your own** balance, based on the length of the recording, even in someone else's workspace.
- **Microphone permission**: the first time, your browser asks for permission. If you refused it, allow the microphone for this site in your browser's settings.

## Web Search

Klecx searches the web on its own when a question needs current or specific information, such as prices, news or recent releases. You don't need to switch anything on.

- **Sources**: answers based on a search show numbered references and a list of sources.
- **Cost**: each search Klecx runs adds {{credits.search}} credits to the answer, paid by the workspace owner like the rest of the answer.
- **Daily limit**: each person can run a number of searches per day in one owner's workspaces, set by that owner's plan: {{plan.FREE.searchesPerDay}} on Free, {{plan.LITE.searchesPerDay}} on Lite, {{plan.STANDARD.searchesPerDay}} on Standard and {{plan.PRO.searchesPerDay}} on Pro. When the limit is reached, Klecx answers without searching and says so in its thought process.
- **Turning it off**: switch off **Search the web when helpful** in **Settings → AI preferences**. Klecx then answers from the model's own knowledge only.
