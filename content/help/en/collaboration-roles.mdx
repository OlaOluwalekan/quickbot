# Collaboration & Roles

You can invite people to a workspace or to a whole project and choose what each person can do.

## Roles

| Action | Viewer | Commenter | Editor | Admin | Owner |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Read the workspace, its documents and shared comments | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Comment, reply and @mention people | ⚙️ off | ⚙️ on | ⚙️ on | ✅ | ✅ |
| Ask Klecx about passages, run the queue, record Klecx notes, request reviews | ⚙️ off | ⚙️ off | ⚙️ on | ✅ | ✅ |
| Send prompts and regenerate answers | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Edit answers and documents, upload files, download | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Rename, archive and create public links | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Invite people and manage members | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Transfer ownership, delete the workspace | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

⚙️ marks a per-member switch: in **Share → People**, the owner or an admin can turn **Can comment** and **Can ask Klecx** on or off for each Viewer, Commenter and Editor. "On" and "off" are the defaults for that role.

## Projects and Workspaces

A role given in a project applies to every workspace in that project. A workspace can give someone more rights than the project does, but never fewer: to lower someone's access, change it on the project.

## Who Pays

Everything Klecx does in a workspace (answers, side questions, reviews, web searches) uses the **workspace owner's** credits. Collaborators don't need credits of their own, except for voice input, which uses the speaker's own credits. In **Share → People**, the owner can set a monthly credit cap for each member.

## Inviting People

1. Open **Share** and enter an email address or a `@username`.
2. Choose a role and click **Invite**.
3. The person receives a single-use invitation link, valid for 7 days. You can resend or revoke pending invitations in the **Invitations** tab.

How many people you can collaborate with depends on your plan: {{plan.FREE.members}} on Free, {{plan.LITE.members}} on Lite, {{plan.STANDARD.members}} on Standard and {{plan.PRO.members}} on Pro. The limit counts **different people across all your workspaces and projects**: inviting the same person to several of your workspaces counts once, and a pending invitation holds a place until it is accepted, revoked or expires. If an owner is over the limit (for example after a plan change), they have 14 days to fix it; after that, the most recently added people become read-only until there is room again. Nothing is deleted.

## Transferring Ownership

The owner can ask another member to take over a workspace or project. The new owner must accept, because their credits will pay for the work from then on, and the transfer must fit their plan's member limit.
