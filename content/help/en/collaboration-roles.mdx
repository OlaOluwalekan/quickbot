# Collaboration & Roles

When working together in Projects or Workspaces, Klecx provides role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure everyone has the exact permissions they need without risking unintended changes.

## Role Breakdown

| Role | Can View | Can Comment | Can Prompt (Generate) | Can Manage Members | Billing Owner |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Viewer** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Commenter** | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Editor** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Admin** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Owner** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

## How Credits are Billed During Team Collaboration

To avoid complex per-seat billing surprises, **all generations within a workspace consume the Owner's credit balance**. Invited collaborators (Editors and Admins) do not need to purchase separate credit packs to collaborate on the project.

## Inviting Team Members

1. Open the project or workspace settings.
2. Enter the email address of your collaborator.
3. Select their initial role.
4. They will receive an invitation email with a secure link to join your workspace.
