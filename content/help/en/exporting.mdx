# Exporting Workspaces

Your research and writing in Klecx belong to you. We provide several clean export formats so you can move your work into knowledge bases, documentation repositories, or client presentations.

## Available Export Formats

1. **Markdown (`.md`)**:
   - Preserves all headers, lists, syntax-highlighted code fences, KaTeX mathematical formulas, and blockquotes.
   - Ideal for pasting into Obsidian, Notion, GitHub wikis, or static site generators.

2. **Portable Document Format (`.pdf`)**:
   - Generates a styled, print-ready document featuring your active response versions, formatted tables, and diagrams.
   - Perfect for formal client deliverables and executive summaries.

3. **Structured Data (`.json`)**:
   - Full serialization of your workspace turns, version branches, timestamps, model parameters, and thinking stage logs.
   - Ideal for programmatic archival, automated workflows, and personal data backups.

## How to Export

Click the **... (More Options)** menu in the upper-right corner of any workspace and choose **Export Workspace**, then select your desired format.
