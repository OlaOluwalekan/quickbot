# Language & Locale Settings

Klecx is built with internationalization at its core. You can customize both the user interface language and the default language the AI uses when responding to your prompts.

## Changing Interface Language

Klecx supports:
- **English (`en`)**
- **Spanish (`es`)**
- **French (`fr`)**

To switch your interface language:
1. Click your avatar in the sidebar and choose **Settings → Language & Appearance**.
2. Alternatively, use the **Locale Switcher** pill located in the header or bottom corner of marketing pages.
3. Your preference is securely stored in your user profile and remembered across devices.

## AI Response Language

By default, modern Gemini models automatically detect the language of your prompt and answer in kind. If you prefer to write prompts in English but always receive answers in Spanish or French, you can specify your **Default AI Language** in **Settings → Language & Appearance**.
