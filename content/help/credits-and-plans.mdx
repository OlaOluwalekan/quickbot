# Credits & Plans

Klecx uses a fair, transparent **credit ledger system**. Unlike flat-rate services that quietly degrade response quality or throttle active users, Klecx ties credits directly to the compute cost of the models you choose.

## Plan Tiers

- **Free Tier**: Includes 100 free monthly credits to explore features and test models. Perfect for light questions and occasional tasks.
- **Pro Plan ($19/month)**: Includes 2,500 credits per billing period, access to Standard Gemini Pro models, increased file attachments, and full project collaboration.
- **Max Plan ($49/month)**: Includes 7,500 credits per billing period, priority model capacity, high-resolution media generation, and dedicated email support.

## How Credits are Deducted

Credits are deducted based on actual input and output tokens consumed during generation:
- **Economy Models (`Gemini 2.5 Flash`)**: Cost 1 credit per ~10k tokens. Extremely cost-effective for everyday chat, summarization, and quick code snippets.
- **Standard Models (`Gemini 2.5 Pro`)**: Cost 1 credit per ~2k tokens. Designed for complex logic, multi-step math, and extensive source documents.
- **Failed Generations**: If a network failure occurs or a generation is interrupted before producing output, reserved credits are immediately released back to your balance.

## Credit Expiry & Refills

Monthly plan credits refresh at the start of each billing period. Subscription credits do not roll over to subsequent months. If you need extra capacity mid-month, you can top up your balance with non-expiring credit packs from your **Settings → Billing** page.
